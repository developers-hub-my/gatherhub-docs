Didn't register beforehand? Scan the poster, fill in a short form and show your ticket QR. No app to install and no password. {% .lead %}

{% callout title="For organisers" %}
Walk-ins need three things: **quick registration** switched on, a **free** ticket type, and the **Event QR** poster at the entrance. See the [organiser guide](/docs/guide-organiser).
{% /callout %}

---

## Step 1: Scan the poster

Point your phone camera at the Event QR at the entrance. The event page opens in your browser.

{% figure src="/images/guides/20-attendee-event-page.jpg" alt="Public event page opened from the Event QR" caption="The event page opens straight from the poster." /%}

---

## Step 2: Pick the free ticket

Under **Get Your Ticket**, tap the free ticket (for example **Walk-in / Guest**, marked **FREE**).

{% figure src="/images/guides/21-attendee-event-page-tickets.jpg" alt="Get Your Ticket list with the free Walk-in / Guest ticket at the bottom" caption="Tap the free ticket." /%}

---

## Step 3: Fill in the short form

The form says **"No account needed — we'll email your ticket to you"**. Enter your full name, email and phone number, tick the consent box if there is one, then tap **Complete Registration**.

{% figure src="/images/guides/22-walkin-quick-form.jpg" alt="Register for Event panel saying no account needed, with full name, email and phone fields and the Complete Registration button" caption="Name, email, phone. That's it." /%}

---

## Step 4: Your ticket appears

**You're registered!** shows your ticket QR and ticket number on screen. A copy goes to your email, with a link to set a password if you want to use your account later.

{% figure src="/images/guides/23-walkin-registered-qr.jpg" alt="You're registered panel with the ticket QR, the ticket number and a note about setting a password" caption="Your ticket QR, on screen and by email." /%}

---

## Step 5: Show the QR to the crew

Registering doesn't check you in by itself. The crew scan your QR at the entrance, the same as everyone else.

---

## Other situations

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Already registered with that email | Tap **Email my ticket again** on the event page |
| Already have an account and signed in | Register as usual; your ticket is also in the app at **gatherhub.app/me** |
| Only paid tickets on sale | Sign in and pay on your phone, or ask the crew to help |
| No crew at the door, or an online event | The organiser shows an **Online QR** on a screen. Scan it to check yourself in |
