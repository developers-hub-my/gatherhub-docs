Set up your event, open registration and get your crew in place. This guide follows the real screens, in order. {% .lead %}

---

## Step 1: Create your account

Go to **my.gatherhub.app/register** and fill in your name, email and password. A personal organisation is created for you, so you can start right away.

{% figure src="/images/guides/01-organiser-register.jpg" alt="Create an account form with name, email, password and confirm password" caption="Create an account." /%}

Open the link in the verification email. Until then you see this screen. Use **Resend verification email** if it hasn't arrived.

{% figure src="/images/guides/02-organiser-verify-email.jpg" alt="Please verify your email address screen with Resend verification email button" caption="Verify your email before you continue." /%}

---

## Step 2: Create the event

Go to **Events → Create**. The wizard has three steps and saves your progress automatically.

**Basic information:** event title, description, start and end date and time, and visibility (Public, Private or Unlisted).

{% figure src="/images/guides/03-organiser-wizard-basic.jpg" alt="Event wizard step 1, Basic Information, with title, description, dates and visibility" caption="Step 1 of the wizard: basic information." /%}

**Location & category:** physical, virtual or hybrid, the venue (from your library or typed in), city, country, category and tags.

{% figure src="/images/guides/04-organiser-wizard-location.jpg" alt="Event wizard step 2, Location and Category, with location type, venue, city, country, category and tags" caption="Step 2: location and category." /%}

**Review & create:** check the summary and tap **Create Event**. The event is saved as a draft, so nobody sees it yet.

{% figure src="/images/guides/05-organiser-wizard-review.jpg" alt="Event wizard step 3, Review and Create, with a summary and a note that the event will be saved as draft" caption="Step 3: review, then Create Event." /%}

You land on **Event Home**. Its setup checklist shows what is left before you can publish.

{% figure src="/images/guides/06-organiser-event-home.jpg" alt="Event Home with stats, the before-the-event checklist and the setup progress card" caption="Event Home: work down the checklist." /%}

---

## Step 3: Add a free ticket for walk-ins

The wizard creates three paid ticket types (Early Bird, Standard, VIP). Edit or delete the ones you don't need. If you expect walk-ins, open **Ticket Types → Add Ticket Type**, name it (for example "Walk-in / Guest"), set the price to **0**, set a capacity and save.

{% figure src="/images/guides/08-organiser-add-free-ticket.jpg" alt="Add ticket type form with name Walk-in / Guest, price 0 and capacity" caption="A free ticket type for walk-ins." /%}

{% figure src="/images/guides/09-organiser-ticket-types.jpg" alt="Ticket Types list with Early Bird, Standard, VIP and a free Walk-in / Guest ticket" caption="The free ticket shows a FREE badge." /%}

{% callout type="warning" title="Check the default prices" %}
The three default tickets come with sample prices (RM50, RM100, RM200). Change or delete them before you publish.
{% /callout %}

---

## Step 4: Turn on quick registration

Open **Details → Registration** and switch on **Quick registration**. Guests on a free ticket can then register with only their name, email and phone. No account or password is needed.

You can also add a **data & privacy notice** here. When you do, attendees must tick a consent box to register.

{% figure src="/images/guides/07-organiser-quick-registration.jpg" alt="Event Details, Registration tab, with the Quick registration switch on and the data and privacy notice field" caption="Details → Registration → Quick registration." /%}

---

## Step 5: Preview and publish

On Event Home, the **Publish** step opens a preview of your public event page. Check it, then tap **Publish Event**. Registration opens and you can share the event link.

{% figure src="/images/guides/10-organiser-preview-publish.jpg" alt="Preview mode banner with Edit Event and Publish Event buttons above the event page preview" caption="Preview mode: Publish Event goes live." /%}

---

## Step 6: Add your crew

Your crew must create their own GatherHub account first. Then go to **Crew Members → Add Crew Member → Invite by Email**, enter their email and pick a role.

| Role | Use for |
|---|---|
| Organizer | Co-organisers: full event access |
| Coordinator | Manages participants and reports |
| Staff | Check-ins and certificates |
| Volunteer | Door scanning only |
| Floor Manager | Live ops on the day (polls, Q&A, sessions) |
| Speaker | Presenter, listed publicly |

{% figure src="/images/guides/12-organiser-add-crew.jpg" alt="Add Crew Member dialog with Invite by Email selected, email, name and role fields" caption="Add Crew Member → Invite by Email." /%}

People who already have an account are added at once and get a notification. Everyone else gets an invitation email and shows under **Pending Invitations** until they accept. You can **Resend** or **Cancel** from there.

{% figure src="/images/guides/13-organiser-crew-pending.jpg" alt="Event Crew page with one pending invitation, and Resend and Cancel buttons" caption="Pending invitations wait here until the crew member accepts." /%}

What your crew see next is in the [crew guide](/docs/guide-crew).

---

## Step 7: Print the Event QR

Open **Details**. Once the event is published, the **Event QR** appears next to the public event link. Download it as **PNG** or **SVG**. It opens your event page and never expires. Put it on the entrance poster and your slides, so walk-ins can register.

{% figure src="/images/guides/11-organiser-event-qr.jpg" alt="Event Details with the public event page link, Copy Event Link, View Event Page and the Event QR with PNG and SVG downloads" caption="The Event QR: permanent, made for printing." /%}

---

## Optional: Online QR for self check-in

The **Online QR** is a different code. It is valid for 1 to 60 minutes and is meant for a screen, not for printing. Use it when there is no crew at the door, or for an online event. Open it from the crew app (**More → Online QR**). It shows a 6-letter code for people without a camera, a countdown, **Extend 5 min** and **Deactivate**.

{% figure src="/images/guides/14-online-qr-present.jpg" alt="Online QR present view with an Active badge, the QR code, Scan to check in and the code YPLSLS" caption="Online QR on a screen." width=300 /%}

{% figure src="/images/guides/14b-online-qr-present-controls.jpg" alt="Online QR with the countdown, Extend 5 min and Deactivate buttons, and the Checked in online list" caption="Extend or deactivate it; check-ins appear below." width=300 /%}

{% callout type="warning" title="Don't print the Online QR" %}
It expires. Print the **Event QR** from Details instead.
{% /callout %}
