Registering before the day means you only need to show your ticket QR at the door. {% .lead %}

---

## Step 1: Open the event page

Use the link the organiser shared. The event page shows the date, venue and organiser.

{% figure src="/images/guides/20-attendee-event-page.jpg" alt="Public event page with the banner, event title, organiser and event details" caption="The event page." /%}

Scroll to **Get Your Ticket** and tap the ticket you want. Free tickets say **Click to register**. Paid tickets say **Login to Register** if you're not signed in.

{% figure src="/images/guides/21-attendee-event-page-tickets.jpg" alt="Get Your Ticket list with Early Bird, Standard, VIP and a free Walk-in / Guest ticket" caption="Get Your Ticket." /%}

---

## Step 2: Fill in your details

Enter your name, email and phone, plus any questions the organiser added. If the event has a privacy notice, tick the consent box. Then tap **Complete Registration**.

{% figure src="/images/guides/24-attendee-registration-drawer.jpg" alt="Register for Event panel with the ticket, number of tickets, discount code and contact details" caption="Register for Event." /%}

- **Free ticket on a quick-registration event:** no account needed.
- **Paid ticket:** you sign in first, so your ticket is saved to your account.

---

## Step 3: Pay (paid tickets only)

On **Complete your order**, choose a payment channel and tap **Pay RM …**.

| Payment channel | What happens |
|---|---|
| FPX Online Banking | Pay from your bank. Confirmed straight away |
| DuitNow QR | Scan with your banking app. Confirmed straight away |
| Bank Transfer | **Submit Order (Pending Approval)**. The organiser confirms it once the money arrives |

{% figure src="/images/guides/25-attendee-order-payment.jpg" alt="Complete your order page with the payment channel, Pay button and order summary" caption="Complete your order. The channels you see depend on the organiser's setup." /%}

If you leave without paying, your spot isn't confirmed. We send you a reminder email so you can pick up where you left off.

Signed in and choosing a free ticket? You land on the same page with **Confirm Free Registration** instead.

---

## Step 4: Get your ticket

Once paid (or registered), the page shows **You're registered!** with your ticket QR, the ticket number and **Download ticket (PDF)**. The same ticket arrives by email.

{% figure src="/images/guides/26-attendee-order-ticket.jpg" alt="You're registered page with the ticket QR, ticket number, Download ticket PDF and order summary" caption="Your ticket, also sent by email." /%}

Signed in? Your ticket is also under the **Ticket** tab of the app at **my.gatherhub.app/me**. Open it once before the day: it then opens offline too.

{% figure src="/images/guides/30-attendee-me-ticket.jpg" alt="My Ticket screen in the attendee app with the QR code, ticket number and ticket type" caption="My Ticket in the app." width=300 /%}

---

## Step 5: Show the QR at the entrance

Turn your screen brightness up and show the QR to the crew. They scan it and you're checked in.

{% callout title="Lost your ticket email?" %}
On the event page, choose **Email my ticket again**. It is only sent to the email you registered with.
{% /callout %}
