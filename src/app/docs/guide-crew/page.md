You've been invited to help run an event. Here is how to get from the invitation email to scanning guests at the door. {% .lead %}

---

## Step 1: Open the invitation

Look for the email **"You're invited to join the crew: …"** and tap **Accept Invitation**. Check your spam folder if it hasn't arrived. The link is valid for 7 days.

The invitation page shows the event, your role and when the invitation expires. Tap **Accept Invitation**.

{% figure src="/images/guides/15-crew-invitation.jpg" alt="You're Invited page showing the event, the role Staff, the expiry date and the Accept Invitation button" caption="The invitation page." /%}

---

## Step 2: Sign up or log in

If you're not signed in, you see **Login Required**. Choose **Create Account** if you're new, or **Log In** if you already have an account.

{% figure src="/images/guides/16-crew-login-required.jpg" alt="Login Required card asking to log in or create an account with the invited email" caption="Log In or Create Account." /%}

On **Create Account**, your invited email is already filled in. Keep it: the invitation only works for that email.

{% figure src="/images/guides/17-crew-register-prefilled.jpg" alt="Create an account form with the invited email already filled in" caption="The invited email is filled in for you." /%}

---

## Step 3: You're on the crew

After you sign up or log in, you come straight back to the invitation and it is accepted for you. Tap **Open crew app**. Verify your email when asked (this happens once).

{% figure src="/images/guides/18-crew-welcome.jpg" alt="Welcome to the Crew page with the role and the Open crew app button" caption="Welcome to the Crew → Open crew app." /%}

---

## Step 4: Get the crew app ready

The crew app lives at **gatherhub.app/ops** and lists every event you crew.

1. Open it on your phone and add it to your home screen from the browser menu.
2. Allow camera access when asked.
3. Open it once on Wi-Fi before the day, so it works with a weak signal.

{% figure src="/images/guides/19-crew-ops-events.jpg" alt="Crew Operations screen listing the events you crew" caption="The crew app lists your events." width=300 /%}

---

## Step 5: On the day, Scan

Open the event and tap **Scan** (the centre button). Point the camera at the attendee's ticket QR. If the camera can't read it, tap **Type code** and enter the ticket number printed under the QR, or use **Find guest** to look the person up by name.

{% figure src="/images/guides/19b-crew-ops-scan.jpg" alt="Check-in scan screen with the camera area, the checked-in counter, and Type code and Find guest buttons" caption="Scan: the counter at the top shows how many are in." width=300 /%}

Scanning opens **1 hour before the event starts**. Before that, the app opens on the **Live** view.

What each scan result means is in [At the gate](/docs/guide-at-the-gate).

---

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No invitation email | Check spam, then ask the organiser to **Resend** it |
| "This invitation was sent to a different email address" | You're signed in with another email. Ask the organiser to invite the email you use |
| Invitation expired | Ask the organiser to invite you again |
| Already have a GatherHub account | The organiser can add you directly. You get a notification and skip steps 1 to 3 |

{% callout title="One account for every event" %}
You only sign up once. Every event you're added to shows in the same crew app.
{% /callout %}
