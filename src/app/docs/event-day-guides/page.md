## Who does what

| Who | Needs an account? | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Organiser | Yes | Creates the event, opens registration, adds crew, prints the Event QR |
| Crew | Yes, created before the organiser adds them | Scans tickets at the entrance with the crew app |
| Pre-registered attendee | Only for paid tickets | Registers or pays before the day, brings the ticket QR |
| Walk-in | No, on a free ticket with quick registration | Scans the poster, fills a short form, shows the QR |

---

## Two ways in, one gate

Crew don't need to know how someone registered. Every attendee arrives with a ticket QR, and the crew scan it the same way.

| Path | Steps | Ends with |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-registered | Event page → register or pay → ticket QR by email | Crew scan at the gate |
| Walk-in | Scan the poster → quick form → ticket QR on the phone | Crew scan at the gate |

What each scan result means, and what to do about it, is in [At the gate](/docs/guide-at-the-gate).

{% callout title="Before the day" %}
Add your crew a few days ahead, so they have time to sign up and accept. Ask them to open the crew app once on Wi-Fi before the event.
{% /callout %}
