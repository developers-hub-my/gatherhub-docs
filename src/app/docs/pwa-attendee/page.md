The attendee view of the GatherHub app puts everything for your event in five tabs along the bottom of the screen: what's happening today, the agenda, your ticket, people to meet, and everything else. {% .lead %}

New to the app? Start with [Getting the app](/docs/pwa-overview#install-the-app).

---

## My events

Open the app outside an event and you see **My events**, grouped into **Happening now**, **Upcoming** and **Past**. Tap an event to open its Home.

{% figure src="/images/pwa/attendee-01-my-events.png" alt="My events screen listing happening now, upcoming and past events" caption="My events: live, upcoming and past events you're registered for." width=300 /%}

When you aren't inside an event, the tabs still point at one event for you: the one happening now, or else your next upcoming event, or else your most recent one. If you have no events yet, you only see **Home**, **Connect** and **Profile**.

## The tabs

| Tab | What's in it |
|-----|--------------|
| **Home** | "Today" for the event: check-in status, what's up next, what's live now, your stats |
| **Agenda** | Sessions, My schedule, Activities, Speakers |
| **Ticket** (centre) | Your ticket, Check in, Kit, Certificates |
| **Connect** | People, Social wall, Stage screen, Contacts, Scan card, My card |
| **More** | A panel with Engage, Content, Rewards, Event and Account |

When a tab has several pages, buttons at the top let you move between them. A page only shows up when the organizer has turned that feature on, so you may see fewer pages than listed here.

## Home: Today

Home changes with the state of the event:

- **Before you check in**, it shows that you haven't checked in yet and how to do it.
- **During the event**, it shows when you checked in, what's **Up next** in your schedule, and a **Live now** list with open polls, Q&A and surveys waiting for you.
- **Before an upcoming event**, it shows the event's date and venue.
- **After the event**, it shows a wrap-up recap.

Your stats (points, badges, rank, sessions, activities and kit) sit at the bottom.

{% figure src="/images/pwa/attendee-02-home-today-checked-in.png" alt="Home Today screen showing checked in time, up next session, live poll, Q&A and survey cards, and stats" caption="Home during a live event: checked in, up next, live now and your stats." width=300 /%}

{% callout title="Open Home once before the event" %}
Open the event's Home while you have internet. The app then keeps your ticket on your phone, so it still opens with no signal at the venue. See [Using the app without internet](/docs/pwa-offline#your-ticket-without-internet).
{% /callout %}

## Agenda

| Page | What you see |
|------|--------------|
| **Sessions** | All sessions of the event |
| **My schedule** | Sessions by day, including the ones you're registered for |
| **Activities** | Activities you can join |
| **Speakers** | Who's speaking |

Sessions and My schedule appear only when the event has sessions. Activities appears only when the event has activities.

## Ticket

The centre tab holds your ticket and everything you need at the door.

### Your ticket

The ticket shows the QR code to present at the entrance, your ticket number, ticket type, check-in time, your details, the event's date and venue, and a **Download Ticket** button.

{% callout title="Show the QR at full brightness" %}
Turn your screen brightness up when a crew member scans your ticket. The QR always sits on a white background, even in dark mode, so it scans reliably.
{% /callout %}

### Check in yourself

Some events let you check yourself in with an event QR code shown at the entrance or on screen.

1. Open **Ticket** and choose **Check in**.
2. Choose **Scan QR** and tap **Enable Camera**, then point your camera at the event QR. If the camera won't work, choose **Enter code** and type the code the organizer shows. You can also scan the event QR with your phone's built-in camera app.
3. If the organizer needs more details from you (for example a company name or a dietary choice), fill them in when asked. Your check-in counts once you save.
4. If the event requires a check-in photo, take a selfie when asked. If photos are optional, you may be offered one after checking in.

{% figure src="/images/pwa/attendee-11-online-check-in.png" alt="Check in screen with Scan QR and Enter code options and a camera prompt" caption="Self check-in: scan the event QR or enter the code." width=300 /%}

{% callout type="warning" title="Checking yourself in needs internet" %}
Scanning the event QR and entering a code both need internet. If there's no signal at the venue, show your ticket to a crew member instead. Crew can scan you without internet.
{% /callout %}

### Kit

If the event hands out a kit (for example a lanyard or a goodie bag), **Kit** shows which items you've collected.

### Certificates

**Certificates** lists the certificates you've earned for the event, with the issue date and status. Tap **Download** to save it, or **Verify** to open the page that proves it's genuine. See [My Certificates](/docs/my-certificates).

{% figure src="/images/pwa/attendee-14-certificates.png" alt="Certificates screen showing a valid certificate of participation with Download and Verify buttons" caption="Certificates for a past event, with Download and Verify." width=300 /%}

## Connect

| Page | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| **People** | Your card and the contacts you've made at this event (when networking is on for the event) |
| **Social wall** | Posts from attendees (when the organizer turns it on) |
| **Stage screen** | Send a short message to the screen on stage |
| **Contacts** | Everyone you've exchanged cards with |
| **Scan card** | Scan another attendee's digital card QR to save them as a contact |
| **My card** | Your digital business card and its QR code |

To swap details with someone, one of you opens **My card** and the other opens **Scan card**.

{% figure src="/images/pwa/attendee-20-connect-my-card.png" alt="My digital business card with QR code" caption="My card: let others scan it to save your details." width=300 /%}

## The More panel

Tap **More** to open a panel with the rest of the event:

| Section | Items |
|---------|-------|
| **Engage** | Polls, Q&A, Surveys |
| **Content** | Recordings, Materials, Gallery |
| **Rewards** | Leaderboard, Badges, Referral |
| **Event** | Event info, Sponsors, Privacy |
| **Account** | My events, Profile, All-events leaderboard |

Each item appears only when the event uses it.

### Polls, Q&A and surveys

- **Polls**: vote on an open poll and see the results.
- **Q&A**: ask the speaker a question. The organizer may review questions before they appear.
- **Surveys**: answer the event's feedback surveys.

Home's **Live now** list tells you when a poll, Q&A or survey is waiting, so you rarely need to look for them.

{% figure src="/images/pwa/attendee-21-engage-polls.png" alt="Polls screen with an active poll" caption="Polls: vote, then see the results." width=300 /%}

### Rewards and leaderboards

On events with points and badges turned on, you earn points for taking part.

- **Leaderboard** ranks attendees of this event, with your own position pinned.
- **Badges** shows the badges you've earned and the ones still locked.
- **Referral** gives you a link to invite others, when the event has referrals turned on.
- **My Stats** and the **All-events leaderboard** (in the avatar menu and under Account) cover all your events.

### Recordings and materials

After the event, **Recordings** and **Materials** list what the organizer has shared. Each item is either free, free for attendees, or paid. A locked item links to the event page where you can buy access.

### Event info, sponsors and privacy

**Event info** has the event details and venue, **Sponsors** lists the event's sponsors, and **Privacy** shows your privacy and consent settings for the event.
