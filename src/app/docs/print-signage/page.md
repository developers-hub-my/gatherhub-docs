Everything you print for the day comes from GatherHub: an A5 vote poster for each booth, and A4 signage that shows visitors how to check in and vote. All of it uses one colour and your logo. {% .lead %}

---

## Colour and Logo

Go to **Run → Print & Signage**. The **Colour & logo** card applies to the signage and every booth QR poster. Changes save straight away.

{% figure src="/images/features/signage-01-print-signage.jpg" alt="Print and Signage page with the colour swatches, organiser logo upload, event-day signage options and a live preview" caption="Run → Print & Signage." /%}

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| **Colour** | GatherHub default, Red, Orange, Green, Teal, Blue, Indigo, Pink, or a custom colour |
| **Organiser logo (optional)** | PNG, JPG or WebP up to 2 MB. A transparent PNG works best. It shows top-right on a white plate |

{% callout title="Light colours are darkened" %}
A very light colour, such as yellow, is darkened automatically so text and the QR stay readable when printed. "Powered by GatherHub" always stays in the footer.
{% /callout %}

## Booth QR Posters

Each booth gets its own A5 poster with its vote QR.

### Choose a poster design

Go to **Run → Vote → QR poster** and click a design. **Preview with** shows the real poster for any booth. Try the one with the longest name.

| Design | Look |
|---|---|
| **Spotlight** (default) | Brand gradient header, big booth number, white body. Light on ink |
| **Night Ink** | Dark poster with a big VOTE. Loudest on the wall, heavy on toner |
| **Ticket Stub** | Clean white top with a tear-off style brand strip |

{% figure src="/images/features/vote-20-poster-tab.jpg" alt="QR poster tab with three design cards and a live A5 poster preview" caption="Run → Vote → QR poster." /%}

### Print or send the posters

Go to **Run → Vote → Overview** and click **Booth QR**. Every booth is listed with its QR, PIC and vote count.

{% figure src="/images/features/vote-21-booth-qr.jpg" alt="Booth QR codes page with Email all PICs, Download all ZIP and Print all buttons and a grid of booth QR cards" caption="Run → Vote → Booth QR." /%}

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Print every poster | **Print all**, then your browser's **Print** (or Save as PDF) |
| Print some posters | Tick the booths, then **Print selected** |
| Get one PDF per booth | **Download all (ZIP)**. We email you a download link when it's ready. The link expires after 24 hours |
| Email every booth its poster | **Email all PICs** |
| Send one booth its poster | **WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the message ready, and you press send. Or use the **⋯** menu |
| Send to anyone else | **⋯** → **Send to any email or number…** (up to 20 emails) |

{% figure src="/images/features/vote-22-poster-sheet.jpg" alt="A5 booth vote poster print sheet in the Spotlight design" caption="The print sheet. One A5 poster per booth." /%}

The poster link a PIC receives works for 30 days without logging in.

{% callout title="Reset QR stops the printed poster" type="warning" %}
**⋯** → **Reset QR** gives a booth a new QR. The poster already on the wall stops working, so reprint it.
{% /callout %}

There is no logo in the centre of the QR on purpose. A logo there stops phones from reading it.

## Event-Day Signage

The **Event-day signage** card on **Print & Signage** builds A4 posters for visitors.

| Poster | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Journey** (A4 landscape) | The day in steps: scan and register, check in, explore the booths, vote. The booth and vote steps appear only when you have active booths and Vote is on |
| **Check-in** | The Event QR for walk-ins, and "show your ticket" for everyone else |
| **Vote** | How to scan a booth and vote. Needs Vote turned on |

You can also choose:

- **Language**: Bahasa Melayu with English sub-lines (default), or English only
- **Wording**: change any title or line. Leave a field empty to use the default shown in it

Click **Save signage**, then **Print signage** to open the print sheet. Each poster prints on its own A4 page.

{% figure src="/images/features/signage-02-sheet.jpg" alt="Event-day signage print sheet with the Journey, Check-in and Vote posters" caption="The signage print sheet." /%}

The Check-in poster uses the permanent **Event QR** (your event page), not the Online QR, so it works all day.
