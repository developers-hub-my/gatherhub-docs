---

## Live Dashboard

Go to **Run → Vote** and click **Live dashboard**. The page refreshes every 5 seconds.

{% figure src="/images/features/vote-10-dashboard.jpg" alt="People's Choice live dashboard with votes cast, visitors voted, exhibitors, turnout, winners by category and the ranking" caption="Run → Vote → Live dashboard." /%}

| Part | Shows |
|---|---|
| Category chips | **All** or one category |
| Figures | **Votes cast**, **Visitors voted**, **Exhibitors** and the share **Of checked-in visitors** who voted |
| **Winners by category** | The leader of each category. A tie shows every booth with a **Tie** badge |
| **Ranking** | Every booth by votes |
| **Latest votes** | The newest votes as they land |

Click **Export CSV** for the results in a spreadsheet.

## Present Mode (Big Screen)

Click **Present mode** to open the board in a new tab for the projector or LED wall. Move the mouse to show the toolbar: pick a category, **Fullscreen** or **Exit**. The toolbar hides by itself.

{% figure src="/images/features/vote-12-present-classic.jpg" alt="Classic presenter: dark live leaderboard with bars, figures and winners by category" caption="The Classic design in Present mode." /%}

### Pick a design

On the **Presenter** tab, click a design. It saves straight away.

| Design | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Classic** (default) | Dark leaderboard with a live feed. Good for a control-room screen |
| **Podium** | Top three on a big podium. Built for the award moment |
| **Race** | Full-width bars that race as votes land. Loudest on an LED wall |
| **Categories** | One column per category with its leader |
| **Daylight** | White and high contrast, for bright halls and weak projectors |

{% figure src="/images/features/vote-11-presenter-tab.jpg" alt="Vote Presenter tab with the five design cards and presenter options" caption="Run → Vote → Presenter." /%}

### Presenter options

| Option | Default |
|---|---|
| **Booths on screen**: Top 5, Top 10 or Top 20 | Top 10 |
| **Rotate categories (seconds)**: cycles All and each category. 0 stays on one view | 0 |
| **Show totals** | On |
| **Show latest votes** | On |
| **Show 'scan to vote' QR** | On |
| **Hide vote counts**: shows the order only, to keep the suspense until the reveal | Off |

Click **Save options** after changing them. The board uses your print colour and logo from [Print & Signage](/docs/print-signage).

### Venue screen link

To run the board on a venue PC without logging in, click **Create link** under **Venue screen link** and open it on that PC. The link is read-only and lasts 7 days. **Revoke all** stops every screen that uses a link.

## Public Leaderboard

Switch on **Show live results to visitors** in **Vote → Settings**. A **Public leaderboard** button appears next to **Save settings**. Share that page with visitors.

{% figure src="/images/features/vote-40-public-leaderboard.jpg" alt="Public booth leaderboard with category chips, winners by category and the ranking" caption="The public leaderboard, only when live results are on." /%}

While the setting is off, the public page doesn't exist.

---

## Crew: Live Votes on the Phone

Crew see the ranking in the [crew app](/docs/pwa-crew): **Live** tab → **Votes**. It shows votes, voters, turnout and the ranking by category, and refreshes every 10 seconds. Voter names are never shown. Crew don't need any setting turned on, only Vote itself.

{% figure src="/images/features/vote-41-crew-live-votes.jpg" alt="Crew app Votes page with vote totals, category chips and the booth ranking" caption="Crew app → Live → Votes." /%}
