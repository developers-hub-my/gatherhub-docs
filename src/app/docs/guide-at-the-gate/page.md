Every attendee arrives with a ticket QR, however they registered. Scan it, read the colour, act. {% .lead %}

---

## Scanning

Open the event in the crew app and tap **Scan**. Point the camera at the ticket QR. The counter at the top shows how many people are in.

{% figure src="/images/guides/19b-crew-ops-scan.jpg" alt="Check-in scan screen with the camera area, the counter, and Type code and Find guest buttons" caption="The Scan screen." width=300 /%}

## What the result means

### Green: Checked in

**"Check-in successful! Welcome to the event."** Let them in, then tap **Scan Next**.

If the organiser turned on check-in photos, you can take one here. Ask the participant first; the photo is deleted 30 days after the event.

{% figure src="/images/guides/27-gate-success.jpg" alt="Green Checked in result with the participant's name, ticket type, ticket number and the Scan Next button" caption="Checked in." width=300 /%}

### Amber: Already in

**"Ticket already checked in."** with the time of the first scan. Usually it's the same person scanned twice. If it's a different person, the ticket was shared: ask the organiser.

{% figure src="/images/guides/28-gate-duplicate.jpg" alt="Amber Already in result showing the time of the first scan and the participant's details" caption="Already in, with the first scan time." width=300 /%}

### Red: Ticket not found

**"Ticket not found for this event."** The QR is for another event, or isn't a ticket. Send them to the walk-in poster, or use **Search by name**.

{% figure src="/images/guides/29-gate-not-found.jpg" alt="Red Ticket not found result with Scan Next and Search by name buttons" caption="Ticket not found." width=300 /%}

### Other results

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Ticket not paid | They can finish paying on their phone from the order page |
| Ticket has been cancelled | Refer them to the organiser |
| Check-in is not open yet | Scanning opens shortly before the event starts. Try again then |
| Wrong gate for this ticket | Send them to the door for their ticket type |

## When there's no QR

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| QR won't scan (cracked screen, glare, printout) | Tap **Type code** and enter the ticket number printed under the QR (for example `TKT-20261010-ABCDE`) |
| No phone or a dead battery | Tap **Find guest** (or open **Guests**), search their name or email and mark them attended |
| No signal | Keep scanning. Scans are saved on the phone and sync when you're back online. Anything that couldn't be applied waits in **More → Sync centre** |
| Not registered at all | Send them to the Event QR poster. See the [walk-in guide](/docs/guide-walk-in) |

{% callout title="Open the app once on Wi-Fi" %}
Before the event, open the crew app once with a good connection. It then keeps working with a weak signal on the day.
{% /callout %}
